Driver Onboarding 司机入驻与使用流程

Start driving in 3 simple steps 简单三步，即刻上线接单

Designed for rapid enterprise deployment with built-in credential security. 专为企业高效部署打造，内置首登强制密码重置等企业级安全机制。

1

Receive Fleet Invite 接收车队邀请与凭证

Your fleet operations manager creates your account in the admin console and assigns your vehicle profile and phone credentials. 车队管理后台为您创建专属账号，并绑定相应运营车辆与手机凭证。

2

First Login Security 首登重置安全密码

Log in using the temporary credentials. DizpatchGO enforces a mandatory password change on first sign-in to protect your profile. 使用初始临时密码登录，系统将强制提示修改个人密码，全面保障账户与数据安全。

3

Go Online & Accept Rides 开启接单与行程履约

Toggle your status to "Online". You'll receive matched assignments with real-time GPS routing, guest language notes, and seamless trip completion. 轻触切换至“接单中”，实时接收智能推荐订单，享受精准定位指引与顺畅履约。

Driver App Highlights 司机端亮点功能

Everything built for on-the-road efficiency 专注于行车途中的便捷与高效

Less administrative friction means your drivers can focus 100% on safety and passenger satisfaction. 减少繁琐的行政操作，让司机能够全心专注于行车安全与乘客优质服务。

Instant Trip Progression 一键推进行程状态

Clear, prominent buttons for "Arrived at Pickup", "Trip Started", and "Completed". No complex multi-screen confirmations. “已到达上车点”、“已接到乘客”、“行程已结束”大按钮清晰易触，无需繁琐层级确认。

In-App Maintenance Reports 应用内故障与保养上报

Notice tire wear or brake issues? Submit a quick maintenance report directly from the app so the workshop can schedule repairs immediately. 发现轮胎磨损或刹车异响？司机可直接在手机端快速提交保养需求，车队工坊即刻跟进。

Passenger Language Matching 语言偏好精准对接

View passenger language badges (English, Mandarin, Malay) before arriving, ensuring courteous and clear communication every trip. 在到达前即可获知乘客语言偏好（英语、中文、马来语），从容提供体贴专业的沟通体验。

Vehicle Compliance Alerts 合规与年检到期提醒

View assigned vehicle road tax, insurance, and Puspakom inspection statuses directly in your profile so you never drive non-compliant vehicles. 个人主页可随时查看所驾车辆的路税、保险与 Puspakom 验车有效期，杜绝违规上路风险。

Trip Summary & Earnings 行程统计与数据回溯

Daily and weekly ride counts, completed distance, and trip history stored transparently in your driver history tab. 每日与每周完成单量、行驶总里程及历史单据透明记录，方便核算。

Strict Privacy & Security 严密隐私与账号安全

GPS location is only shared when toggled "Online". Passenger contact numbers are protected through secure internal dispatch channels. 仅在“上线”状态下同步定位；乘客联系方式通过内部安全调度通道保护，严守隐私。

Common Questions 常见问题解答

Frequently Asked Questions for Drivers 司机常见问题

Got questions about using the DizpatchGO Driver App? We've got answers. 关于使用 DizpatchGO 司机端应用的常见疑问与解答。

How do I get my login credentials? 我该如何获取司机端登录账号？

Your fleet administrator will create your profile in the DizpatchGO admin portal and supply your initial login phone number and password. 您的车队运营管理员会在后台为您配置专属账号，并将初始账号及临时密码发放给您。

Does the app track my location when I am offline? 下线或休息时，App 还会追踪我的定位吗？

No. GPS location tracking is strictly active only when you switch your toggle to "Online" or during an active ongoing trip assignment. 不会。GPS 位置同步仅在您主动开启“接单中”或处于执行订单期间启用，充分保护司机私人休息时间。

Can I report vehicle mechanical issues in the app? 如果车辆出现故障，可以直接在 App 内报修吗？

Yes. The app has a dedicated "Submit Maintenance" feature where you can select issue categories (brakes, tires, engine, air conditioning) and enter notes for your fleet workshop. 可以。App 提供便捷的“保养报修”功能，您可直接选择故障类型（刹车、轮胎、发动机、空调等）并提交备注，直接同步给车队后勤。

What devices are supported? 支持哪些手机与操作系统？

DizpatchGO Driver works smoothly on modern Android and iOS smartphones with responsive, lightweight battery-optimized performance. DizpatchGO 司机端全面适配主流 Android 与 iOS 智能手机，经过深度功耗优化，省电流畅。
