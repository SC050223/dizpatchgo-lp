Billing FAQ 计费与订阅疑问

Frequently Asked Questions about Pricing 常见计费与开通问题解答

Everything you need to know about the setup fee, promo rates, quotas, and expansion. 关于初始配置费、首年特惠、配额计算以及增购扩容的详细说明。

What does the RM 3,000 one-time setup fee cover? 一次性 RM 3,000 的初始配置费包含哪些服务？

The setup fee covers full initial tenant provisioning, custom dispatch rule configuration, vehicle and driver database batch migration, and dedicated hands-on administrator training for your dispatchers. 该费用包含云端独立租户部署、车队调度规则深度定制、历史车辆与司机档案批量迁移导入，以及针对您调度团队的专属系统实操培训。

How does the 1st Year Promo annual license work? 首年特惠年度许可证（First Year Promo）如何运作？

When choosing an annual license, your first year is discounted significantly (Basic: RM 2,000 instead of RM 3,000; Pro: RM 4,000 instead of RM 6,000; Premium: RM 8,000 instead of RM 12,000). Subsequent renewals are billed at the standard annual rate. 选择年度许可证时，首年可享受大幅折扣（基础版 RM 2,000，原价 RM 3,000；专业版 RM 4,000，原价 RM 6,000；高级版 RM 8,000，原价 RM 12,000）。次年续订将按标准年费计算。

What happens if we exceed the monthly orders or registered vehicles? 如果当月订单量或注册车辆超出套餐配额会怎样？

You can add extra vehicles anytime at the tiered rate (Basic: RM300/veh, Pro: RM225/veh, Premium: RM150/veh). If your monthly order volume surges, you can upgrade your plan immediately with prorated billing. 您可以随时按优惠费率增购额外车辆配额（基础版 RM 300/车，专业版 RM 225/车，高级版 RM 150/车）。若单月订单量大幅增长，也可随时平滑升级至更高方案，费用按天折算。

Can we purchase additional driver seats without upgrading the whole plan? 可以在不升级整个套餐的情况下单购司机或管理席位吗？

Yes. Each tier offers dedicated add-on seat pricing (e.g. Pro plan driver seat at RM 40/seat/mo and admin seat at RM 50/seat/mo), giving your fleet maximum flexibility as staffing changes. 可以。各方案均提供单座增购单价（如专业版司机账号 RM 40/席/月，管理席位 RM 50/席/月），让车队能根据人员编制变动灵活配置。
