Core Features核心功能

Everything your fleet operations need 您的车队运营所需的一切

From real-time dispatch to compliance monitoring — DizpatchGO gives operations teams complete control and visibility. 从实时调度到合规监控——DizpatchGO 让运营团队拥有完全的控制权和可视性。

  • Smart Dispatch Engine

    智能调度引擎

    AI-powered driver suggestions ranked by GPS proximity, language match, and seating capacity. Assign the best driver in seconds.

    AI驱动的司机推荐，按GPS距离、语言匹配和座位容量排名。几秒内指派最佳司机。

  • Full Order Lifecycle

    完整订单生命周期

    Manage orders from creation through pending, assigned, ongoing, and completed — with cancellation, reassignment, and audit trails.

    管理从创建到待处理、已分配、进行中和完成的全过程，支持取消、重新分配和审计追踪。

  • Fleet & Compliance

    车队与合规管理

    Track road tax, insurance, and Puspakom expiry dates across your entire fleet. Get alerts before anything lapses.

    追踪整个车队的道路税、保险和车检到期日期，在任何事项失效前获得警报。

  • Real-Time GPS Tracking

    实时GPS追踪

    Drivers share live location data that feeds directly into the smart dispatch algorithm. Always know where your fleet is.

    司机共享实时位置数据，直接输入智能调度算法。随时了解您的车队位置。

  • Multilingual Matching

    多语言匹配

    Match clients to drivers by preferred language — English, Bahasa Malaysia, or Chinese — for a smoother experience on every trip.

    根据客户首选语言——英语、马来语或中文——匹配司机，让每次出行更顺畅。

  • Maintenance Tracking

    维护追踪

    Log service history, track mileage, receive overdue alerts, and manage traffic summons — all in one place.

    记录服务历史、追踪里程、接收过期警报并管理交通传票——全部在一处管理。

How It Works工作原理

Dispatch made effortless 轻松实现调度

Three simple steps from order creation to trip completion. 从订单创建到行程完成，三个简单步骤。

  1. 1

    Create an Order

    创建订单

    Admin enters the client details, pickup & dropoff locations, transport type, and scheduled time. Google Maps autocomplete makes it fast.

    管理员输入客户详情、取送地点、运输类型和预定时间。谷歌地图自动完成让操作更快捷。

  2. 2

    Smart Assign a Driver

    智能指派司机

    DizpatchGO ranks the nearest available drivers by real driving time, language match, and capacity. Assign in one click.

    DizpatchGO 按实际驾驶时间、语言匹配和容量对最近的可用司机进行排名。一键指派。

  3. 3

    Track & Complete

    追踪与完成

    The driver accepts the order, starts the trip, and marks it complete. Admins see live status updates across all active orders.

    司机接受订单、开始行程并标记完成。管理员可看到所有活跃订单的实时状态更新。

Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

Got questions about DizpatchGO? We've got answers.关于 DizpatchGO 的常见疑问与解答。

What is DizpatchGO?什么是 DizpatchGO？

DizpatchGO is a fleet dispatch management platform for enterprise operations, with driver matching, GPS tracking, order management and fleet compliance tracking.DizpatchGO 是面向企业运营的车队调度管理平台，提供司机匹配、GPS 追踪、订单管理和车队合规追踪功能。

How does DizpatchGO assign drivers?DizpatchGO 如何指派司机？

DizpatchGO ranks available drivers using a scoring algorithm based on driving time, language compatibility and vehicle capacity.DizpatchGO 使用评分算法，根据驾驶时间、语言兼容性和车辆容量对可用司机进行排名。

Can DizpatchGO track vehicle compliance?DizpatchGO 可以追踪车辆合规情况吗？

Yes. It tracks vehicle road tax, insurance and Puspakom inspection expiry dates and alerts teams before they lapse.可以。它可追踪车辆道路税、保险和 Puspakom 车检的到期日期，并在到期前提醒团队。
